# Imagery Attributions

All imagery used in this project is drawn from NASA's public image archives.
Per [NASA's media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/),
NASA content is generally not protected by copyright and is free for public
use. No generic sci-fi or third-party stock imagery is used, and no
non-NASA imagery is presented as archival evidence.

---

## AS11-40-5875 — Archive landing hero background

- **Title (NASA):** "Astronaut Edwin Aldrin poses for photograph beside deployed U.S. flag"
- **Date:** 20 July 1969 — Apollo 11 extravehicular activity, Tranquility Base
- **Photographer:** Neil A. Armstrong, 70 mm Hasselblad lunar surface camera
- **Credit:** NASA/JSC
- **Description (NASA):** Aldrin poses beside the deployed United States flag
  during the Apollo 11 EVA. The Lunar Module is on the left; astronaut
  footprints are clearly visible in the lunar soil.
- **Source page:** https://images.nasa.gov/details/as11-40-5875
- **Asset used:** https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/as11-40-5875/as11-40-5875~large.jpg
  (JPEG, 1856 × 1920, `~large` rendition of the NASA original)
- **Local copy:** `src/assets/hero/as11-40-5875-large.jpg`
- **Usage & treatment:** Full-viewport hero background on the archive landing
  page. Displayed with interface gradient overlays, desaturation and a slow
  CSS drift; no content of the photograph itself is altered.

## JSC2008-E-040725 — Featured expedition panorama

- **Title (NASA):** Panorama view of Apollo 11 lunar surface photos taken by
  Astronaut Neil Armstrong at Tranquility Base of a crater Armstrong noted
  during the Lunar Module descent
- **Date:** assembled 20 May 2008 from Apollo 11 frames **AS11-40-5954
  through AS11-40-5961** (photographed during the 20 July 1969 EVA)
- **Credit:** NASA/JSC (panorama assembly: Harnett)
- **Description (NASA):** Panorama built by combining Apollo 11 surface
  frames with minimal retouching by NASA imagery specialists (lens-flare
  removal, sky blackout). Shows the crater near the LM descent path with the
  Lunar Module "Eagle" at left.
- **Source page:** https://images.nasa.gov/details/jsc2008e040725
- **Asset used:** https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/jsc2008e040725/jsc2008e040725~large.jpg
  (JPEG, 1920 × 547, `~large` rendition of the NASA original)
- **Local copy:** `src/assets/hero/jsc2008e040725-large.jpg`
- **Usage & treatment:** Featured expedition panel on the archive landing
  page. Displayed with interface gradient overlays and desaturation; the
  on-page caption identifies it as a site panorama assembled from the listed
  frames.

## Labelling policy

- Genuine NASA archive imagery is always labelled with its frame/ID and
  "NASA/JSC" in the interface.
- Any future artistic reconstruction, diagram or illustration will be
  explicitly labelled as a reconstruction — never presented as historical
  evidence.
- No telemetry values, mission statistics or survey data are fabricated for
  the interface; readouts report the application's own build state only.
